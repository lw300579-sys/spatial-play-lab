# Bio-Tactical Edge golden-rally acceptance checklist

Status: not yet published

The public demonstration is complete only when every item below can be inspected from one page.

- [ ] One unedited, publishable 20-second source clip with provenance
- [ ] Visible court calibration anchors and confidence
- [ ] Player and ball tracks with failure/occlusion markers
- [ ] Source video, court coordinates, and 3D reconstruction on one scrubber
- [ ] One tactical question declared before the final result is inspected
- [ ] One answer with uncertainty and direct links to supporting frames
- [ ] One-page export with method, evidence, limitations, and date
- [ ] Ground-truth or reviewer notes and a reconstruction-error definition
- [ ] Privacy, retention, deletion, and model-transmission statement
- [ ] Known failure case shown beside the successful rally

Do not replace an unchecked item with a simulated or inferred success.
